ChatGPT writes well. Corporate reporting fails for other reasons: wrong numbers, missing approvals, version confusion, and compliance gaps.

## ChatGPT drafts text

Give it a prompt, get a paragraph. Useful for a chairman's letter first pass — dangerous when figures aren't tied to source systems.

## A reporting workflow owns the cycle

A workflow platform is designed to:

- Connect ERP, Excel, and BI sources
- Run validation before narrative work
- Route sections to owners with deadlines
- Track every revision for audit readiness

**AI doesn't write your report. It builds your reporting workflow.**

## Where teams win

Founding teams we talk to care less about eloquence and more about cutting review cycles from weeks to days — without surprises in the final board pack.
