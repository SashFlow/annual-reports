Corporate annual reports still run on email threads, version chaos, and spreadsheets that never quite reconcile. The cycle doesn't have to take twelve weeks.

## Start with validation, not narrative

Most teams open Word before numbers are clean. Flipping the order — reconcile ERP, Excel, and BI exports first — surfaces errors when they're cheap to fix.

## Assign sections early

Route each chapter to an owner with a deadline before drafting begins. Parallel ownership beats sequential rewrites.

## Treat the workflow as the product

AI that only drafts paragraphs saves an afternoon. AI that orchestrates collection, validation, review, and compliance saves a quarter.
